## Headline

**148 defects on 17/17 pages (28 HIGH, 69 MED, 51 LOW). 15 calculators gave wrong results for some inputs.** All were worked on. The most serious:

- **Birthday and age pages:** lottery ticket at 16 (it is 18), marriage rules wrong by nation, National Living Wage from 25 (it is 21), Winter Fuel "from 60" and Pension Credit only, wrong royal message rules, and the animal sign ignoring lunar new year. The same age errors were on the age page.
- **Paint:** the example left the reader 0.9 L short, and there was no warning about dry-sanding old lead paint.
- **Wallpaper:** the widget ignored the cut allowance the page itself described: 9 rolls where 12 are needed for a 5×4×2.5 m room. A repeat typed in centimetres showed "Infinity" rolls.
- **Gravel:** told people to build permeable drives on Type 1, which government guidance says stops water passing unless it drains to a soakaway or rain garden.
- **Topsoil and mulch:** prices were about a quarter to a half of current listings (bulk bag "£35 to £80", listed from about £110), and raised bed depths conflicted with RHS. Bulk bags were called cheaper when listings showed the reverse.
- **Lawn seed:** full use after 8 to 12 weeks; RHS says use as little as possible for about eight months.
- **Fence paint:** 86.4 m² at 9 m²/L when the page's own rough-timber figure was 4 to 6.
- **Prime numbers:** large inputs could hang the calculation at 2^53.
- **Binary:** silently misread "1012", "FG" and spaced input, and lost precision on 64-bit values.
- **Roman numerals:** accepted IC, VX and MMMM, though the page said nothing else is allowed.
- **Exponent:** 10^-11 displayed as 0.
- **Random numbers:** -10 to 0 drew up to 100, and a huge range could freeze the tab.
- **Number to words:** -1.5 became "Minus Two", and 1.999 became "one pound and one hundred pence".
- **Volume:** 750 ml bottle "6 medium glasses" at 175 ml (it is 4.3).

Calculators with wrong results: flooring, gravel, wallpaper, lawn-seed, topsoil, mulch, fence-paint, number-to-words, random-number-generator, birthday, square-root, binary-converter, roman-numeral-converter, prime-number, exponent.

## Known limits

- **Prices** on garden pages are retailer listings on 1 November 2026, some marketplace; delivery is extra, and loose-load prices stay unverified.
- **Assumptions labelled on the page:** topsoil 1.3 t/m³; gravel 1.6 t/m³ (one supplier's derivation); £95/t and £150/t defaults.
- **Fence paint:** Ronseal says up to 6 m²/L, retailers list 3; the page shows both and says to check the tin.
- **Wallpaper:** the 10 cm allowance rests on a small DIY site, because B&Q and Graham & Brown would not load.
- **Paint:** the test kit suggestion is not from HSE. The flooring byline still names building regulations; its materials prices were not in scope.
- **Lunar table** covers 1924 to 2043, taken from Hong Kong Observatory, because Node's calendar was a day out for some years. Dates outside it, in the window around new year, show "Not available".
- **Not checked:** BBC source links on maths pages, which the site blocks; Rocky II and Rambo III.
