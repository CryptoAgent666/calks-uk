## Headline

**About 120 defects on 20/20 pages; 15 calculators gave wrong answers for realistic inputs.** The core pages
were not safer than the long tail. The income tax calculator called pay after income tax "take-home" (£42,514 on
£50k; real take-home £39,519.60). SSP underpaid any absence that was not a whole week, sometimes by half. The
Universal Credit calculator gave every eldest child the pre-2017 rate and never applied the benefit cap.

## Lesson

The highest-traffic pages had been through every automated audit since May and several data sweeps, and still
had the most consequential bugs of any round: a "take-home" figure without NI, SSP for short absences, UC without
the benefit cap. The data sweeps check numbers; nobody had typed a two-day absence into the sick pay calculator.
Hand-working one edge input per calculator keeps paying off on "core" pages too.
