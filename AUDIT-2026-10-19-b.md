# CalkCheck Audit — calks.uk — 19 October 2026, round 10

**Locale:** en-GB · GBP · gov.uk / legislation.gov.uk / judiciary.uk / hmrc manuals
**Mode:** sample, 20 never-hand-checked calculators in the regulated categories that remained: tax (self-assessment,
company car, VAT flat rate, tax code checker, tax refund), pay (redundancy, mileage allowance, notice period, work
from home relief, overtime, agency worker, settlement agreement), benefits (child maintenance, childcare entitlement),
legal (employment tribunal, personal injury), immigration (spouse visa), pension (employer contribution), business
(R&D tax credit), savings (Help to Save).

## Headline

**105 defects on 20/20 pages (12 HIGH, 29 MED, 64 LOW); 8 calculators gave wrong answers for realistic inputs and
5 more described inputs or outputs they did not have.** The biggest law change missed by the site: the employment
tribunal time limit became **6 months** on 1 October 2026 (Employment Rights Act 2025 s.152, SI 2026/954), still
stated as 3 months on five pages. Other catches: the tax code checker rejected every K, S and C code and the
emergency markers (three of its own nine quick-pick buttons returned "not recognised"); the VAT flat-rate page quoted
management consultancy at 12% and real estate at 10% (both 14%) and had no 16.5% limited-cost rate, so it could
never show a loss; the tribunal calculator ignored the 52-weeks'-pay limb of the compensatory cap; Help to Save was
made permanent on 21 September 2026 while the page said "until April 2027"; the CMS calculator had no equal-care
band (50% plus £7 a week per child) and no £7 floor; the childcare calculator funded 30 hours for every household.

Confirmed at source (and corrected in the reviewer brief and a memory note): the 2026/27 unfair-dismissal
compensatory cap is **£123,543** (SI 2026/310, was £118,223), not the £123,785 a June memory note had recorded.
The site already had £123,543. The employee working-from-home deduction really was abolished from 6 April 2026
(FA 2026 s.21 inserting s.360B ITEPA); the page built on 13 September is right.

## Method notes

Four read-only reviewers (5 pages each, hand-working two inputs per calculator against the law, fetching gov.uk and
legislation.gov.uk directly because the local search engine was rate-limited), lead re-verification of every HIGH and
of the market/policy facts, then four Edit-only editors in the same checkout (no clobbering across 22 files), lead
sweep for out-of-sample copies, guards, ledger, build, Playwright on the dist build. A reviewer brief carried one
wrong figure from memory (£123,785); checking the SI before briefing would have avoided a mid-run correction.
