## Lesson

The ledger was right far more often than the prose: HVCTS bands, Band D figures, Plan 4 threshold, LEL, VED and
the APR/BPR cap were all correct in `canonical.json` and wrong on the page. Market figures (mortgage rates) are
not in the ledger at all, and they moved the most: 1.5 points since the text was written.
