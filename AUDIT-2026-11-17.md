## Headline

**62 defects on 20/20 pages (4 HIGH, 23 MED, 35 LOW).** Fewer than rounds 19 and 20, which fits pages that had a logic check before; but every page still had something. All were worked on. The most serious:

- **Inheritance Tax:** no way to add the nil-rate and residence bands passed on by a late spouse. A widow(er) with a £900,000 estate and a £400,000 home left to children saw £160,000 of tax; the answer is £0.
- **Bonus tax:** no Scottish rates. A Scottish taxpayer on £45,000 with a £5,000 bonus saw £1,000 of tax instead of £2,100. The NI 2029 page had the same gap in its relief figures.
- **Wealth growth:** the worked example contradicted the page's own calculator by £255,000 at year 30, and the prose described features that don't exist.
- **Student allowance:** household income did nothing, and the example said £8,690 at £30,000 income (2026/27: £10,058) with a total that still used the 2025/26 fee.
- **Care cost:** NHS-funded nursing care (£267.68 a week) was missing, so council and self-funder shares were overstated; no 12-week property disregard; England only.
- **Car import duty:** many 0% origin partners were charged 10% (Mexico, Canada, Australia and more), diesel ignored RDE2, and EU-registered cars paid an IVA they don't need.
- **Citizenship:** said "may be eligible" without the Life in the UK test or English.
- **Water:** the unmetered estimate was a straight line through £639 × RV/200 with no source.
