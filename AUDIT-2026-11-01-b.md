## Headline

**132 defects on 20/20 pages (20 HIGH, 59 MED, 53 LOW). 13 calculators gave wrong results for some inputs, and most of the others
described inputs or outputs they did not have.** All were worked on. The most serious:

- **Macros:** a 45 kg woman was given a 612 kcal target; extreme inputs gave negative calories; it accepted age 10; default protein was 2.5 g/kg.
- **Protein:** said the limits on high intake were "appetite and cost". UK guidance advises avoiding more than 1.5 g/kg (2 x RNI).
- **Sleep:** labelled 6 hours "Good" and 4.5 hours "Minimum". NHS: adults need 7 to 9 hours.
- **Waist-to-hip:** widget and prose used different bands; a man at 0.95, above the WHO cut-off, was "Moderate".
- **GPA:** invented GPA-to-class cut-offs. 60 credits of First and 60 of Third gave a 2:1, where marks of 70 and 40 average 55%, a 2:2.
- **Extension:** permitted depth "6 m detached" (it is 4 m, or 8 m with prior approval); fee £258 (it is £548); and value added jumped at 21 m²,
  giving +326% return on a 5 m² room.
- **Staircase:** 2R+G was left out of the verdict, so stairs at 740 and 520 passed; handrail and width rules were wrong.
- **Employee cost:** ignored Employment Allowance, overstating a small firm's first hire by £4,500.
- **Radiators:** the External Walls input did nothing, and the page said ΔT50 is what a radiator gives on a 50-60°C boiler (about half at 55/45).
- **Concrete:** 0.6 m³ was "about 40 bags", a 20-27% shortfall; driveways used the wrong mix (PAV1 not mentioned).
- **Shoe size:** the formula gave UK 29 for a 270 mm foot, and child sizes snapped silently to adult ones.
- **Probability:** Premium Bonds and Lotto odds were out of date.

Calculators with wrong results: employee-cost-breakdown, cash-flow, employer-pension-contribution, first-homes-scheme, night-shift, extension-cost,
staircase, macro, waist-hip-ratio, sleep, gpa, radiator-btu, shoe-size-converter. Probability's "conditional" and "mutually exclusive" claims and
concrete's bag counts were prose against widget, counted as defects, not in the 13.

## Known limits

- **Macro protein:** 1.0 and 1.2 g/kg are working figures, and the page says so; only 1.6 g/kg has a source (Morton). The 1,200 kcal floor is the upper figure of the NHS's "under 800 or 1200".
- **Protein caution** shows on the default (75 kg, moderately active), because its top figure of 1.6 g/kg exceeds 1.5.
- **Extension:** flat uplift means a very small room still looks profitable (5 m² gives +74%); the double-storey rate is at the bottom of Checkatrade's range, and the page says so. Example total rises to £82,416 now that VAT and contingency count.
- **Weak sources:** wrap-around rates and fee percentages (BestBuilders); FSB 65.8% (via Retail Banker International); GPA scale read via Wayback; Lotto from Allwyn, because national-lottery.co.uk returned nothing.
- **Derived and disclosed on the page:** plastering midpoint, EU half sizes, 25 kg bag yield.
- **Left in, unverified:** 153 BTU/m³, Type 11 920 W, curing percentages, patio labour costs, some employee cost estimates, £ protein costs, the December 2024 NPPF wording on First Homes.
- **Kitchen:** Mid-Range, Premium and Luxury tiers replaced by Checkatrade's small, average and large.
