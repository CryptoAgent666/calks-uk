## Headline

**130 defects on 20/20 pages (17 HIGH, 54 MED, 59 LOW).** All were worked on. The most serious:

- **Visa points:** added up ticks, so people who would be refused saw "eligible". The worked example passed a developer on £38,000, where SOC 2134 needs £54,700, or £38,300 at the deepest discount. Any PhD got the STEM rate. It now takes the occupation and salary, and names what fails.
- **ILR:** counted from UK entry, including Student and Graduate time, and applied the 180-day test to Spouse and Parent routes, which Appendix Continuous Residence does not cover. The 28-day rule was missing.
- **Inflation:** claimed to divide CPI index values but compounded a flat 3.5%, giving £236 for £100 from 2000 to 2025 (CPI: £190.37). The salary example used an index figure that isn't on the current base (£39,776; correct £50,843). It now holds ONS CPI and RPI data.
- **Making Tax Digital:** property income was the number of properties times £8,000, and one figure decided the start date. A landlord on £60,000 rent was told April 2028 instead of April 2026.
- **MTD readiness:** "well prepared" without software or sign-up, which are mandatory.
- **Business mileage:** "You profit from HMRC rates: +£2,808" whatever the employer pays. 55p is a ceiling.
- **Council Tax:** second homes "200% premium" (up to double the bill), empty homes from two years (one year, up to 4x at 10 years), and a "local authority" estimate with no local input.
- **Remortgage:** ignored time left on the fix, so an ERC could look worth paying when it loses about £2,000.
- **Farm costs:** £89/t called the figure to hold against the market price, though it left out rent and labour (£131.25/t on the widget's defaults).

## Known limits

- **Visa points** holds 14 SOC codes, with manual entry for others and for health rates. The Budget 2026 documents announce threshold changes without figures.
- **Moneyfacts:** only the mortgage rate and remortgage pages moved to 2 November figures. About 20 other copies say "start of October", which stays true as dated. Roll them after the 5 November Bank decision.
- **EPC:** costs come from EST, but widget score weights are the site's own. EST's loft, cavity and solar savings would not render, so the page says "varies" or gives payback.
- **BMI:** the warning band of 10 to 60 is a typing-slip check, not sourced. NCMP figures were removed because the source is behind a CAPTCHA.
- **Teacher pay** take-home excludes student loans, and says so.
- **ILR:** "if you arrived later, use your arrival date" is cautious wording, not a Rules statement.
- **Left unverified on council tax and EPC:** care leavers exemption, "85% pick a fix", "typical home Band D", lender stance on F and G, deprivation claim.
- **Citizenship timeline** still totals "tests £210", as test prices were not verified this round.
- **Rollover:** `LATEST_INDEX_MONTH` needs rolling at each ONS release (next 18 November); the smart meter widget adds a standing charge input and label to the 1 January Ofgem list.
