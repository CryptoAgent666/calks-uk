## The 4 per-page failures — all EAT-1 false positives

All four report `Gov sources: 0`, yet every page **does** cite an authoritative
regulator, and the link renders correctly in `<main>` (verified on the live site):

| Page | Actual source rendered |
|---|---|
| employers-liability | `hse.gov.uk` — Health and Safety Executive |
| electricity-cost | `ofgem.gov.uk` — Ofgem, the energy regulator |
| debt-free | `moneyhelper.org.uk` — government-backed MoneyHelper |
| binary-converter | `bbc.co.uk/bitesize` — appropriate for a utility page |

**Root cause (new):** the EAT-1 pattern does not credit **gov.uk sub-domains**.
`hse.gov.uk` and `ofgem.gov.uk` are genuine UK regulators and should count. This
extends the previously documented EAT-1 false positive, which only covered
utility calculators lacking gov links.

No rewrite warranted; changing these would make the content worse, not better.
