## Lessons

1. **Logic bugs dominated this round.** Rounds 1–2 were mostly stale prose; here 12 of 20 calculators computed
   wrong answers (tax bands, pay-period NI, pension basis, taper order, double discounts, APR conversion, a
   rolling-window test). Reviewers found them by working 1–2 inputs by hand against the law. This needs doing
   on every page, not only the ones with suspicious prose.
2. **"Applied" in the ledger covered the engine only.** The 29 Aug phase-4 fix set car import duty to 10% in
   the code, and the Updates page announced it. The button label, three prose paragraphs, the worked example
   and the FAQ still said 6.5% a month later.
3. **The ledger was right and the prose wrong again:** visa fees, water £639, State Pension £241.30 and probate
   £526 were all current in `canonical.json`.
