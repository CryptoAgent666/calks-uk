## Lessons

1. **Shared helpers hide shared bugs.** The Scottish-band error sat in three components written separately;
   one reviewer spotted it on the comparison page, a grep for the band constants found the other two, and
   the one component built on band widths (tax refund, emergency tax) was already right.
2. **The ledger can be wrong in the other direction too:** `hicbc_household_income_basis_date` recorded a
   scrapped policy as current, which is exactly what the prose said.
3. **Market inputs move fast:** petrol rose 10p in September 2026 alone. Hard-coded pump prices need a date on
   every copy and a refresh each cycle.
