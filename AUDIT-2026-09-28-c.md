## Lesson

"APR" is a legal term in the UK, not just a label: the monthly rate has to come from the effective annual
rate. Five calculators had copied the US nominal convention, which is invisible at 7% and badly wrong at 40%.
The property pages had the opposite problem to earlier rounds: instead of stale figures, they reported
announced reforms as law.
