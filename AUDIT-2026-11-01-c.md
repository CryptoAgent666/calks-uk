## Headline

**148 defects on 20/20 pages (18 HIGH, 77 MED, 53 LOW). 18 calculators gave wrong results for some inputs.** All were worked on; one is only
partly fixed (see Known limits). The most serious:

- **Roof tiles:** doubled the roof area, so a roof took twice the tiles, battens and felt (8×5 m: 1,026 tiles instead of 513). Plain clay tiles were undercounted 4×.
- **Sole trader:** no Scottish bands (£55,000 understated by £1,650.05), and "entertainment only if it is for clients", when gov.uk says you cannot usually claim it.
- **Business rates:** a £0 or blank last bill switched off the Supporting Small Business cap (£4,125.33 or £6,272 instead of £800); the cap was applied after pub relief; pub relief had an RV limit the guidance does not have; Scottish island relief was missing.
- **Time zones:** fixed offsets, despite claiming to follow daylight saving. On 1 November, London 14:00 showed Sydney 00:00 instead of 01:00.
- **Trigonometry and logarithms:** tan 90° showed 1.63e+16, asin(2) showed 90°, and 10^7 displayed as 1, because a formatter stripped significant zeros.
- **Mean, median, mode:** "1,000, 2,000" was read as four numbers with a mean of 0.75.
- **Bricks:** wrong party wall rules (2 months for excavation, where it is 1 month and only if deeper than the neighbour's foundations).
- **Plaster:** PVA advice and a 1 to 2 day wait, where British Gypsum requires Bond-it on painted or tiled surfaces.
- **Paving:** said back gardens are fine "below 50% coverage". Class F has no such limit.
- **Greenhouse:** "building regulations" limit of 25 m², with no basis; float bug cost a plant at the default size.
- **Raised bed:** listed railway sleepers with no warning, though RHS says creosoted ones are no longer permitted in the garden.
- **Split bill:** any service charge could be removed "for any reason"; Which? says a compulsory one may need proof of very poor service. £50 ÷ 3 summed to £50.01.
- **Steps:** said 150 minutes equals 7,000 to 8,000 steps a day; at the page's own rate it is about 2,100.
