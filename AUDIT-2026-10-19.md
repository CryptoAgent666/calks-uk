## Method

Site-wide + per-page + AI scripts (`audit_batch.py`, then `audit_page.py`/`audit_ai_content.py` on the ten URLs the batch's default limit skipped); rendered `<main>` text of all 20 pages extracted with cache-bust; four read-only reviewers (5 pages each) with the memory notes on known false positives and defect shapes; lead re-verified the market facts (ONS income FYE 2025, ONS rents Aug 2026, ABI 2024 claims) and the DfE/STA table diffs; three editors applied fixes with the Edit tool only; stale-value guards added for every replaced figure; `npm run check:stale` and a full build after.
