## Headline

**110 defects on 20/20 pages (22 HIGH, 45 MED, 43 LOW). 14 calculators gave wrong results for some inputs.** All were
worked on; one is only partly fixed (see Known limits). The most serious:

- **Personal injury:** the whiplash tariff was treated as a neck-only matter and applied to any road user. Civil Liability
  Act 2018 s.1 covers soft tissue of the neck, back or shoulder, for users of a motor vehicle other than a motorcycle. A car
  occupant with a soft-tissue back injury saw £3,240–£5,750 instead of £1,510; a cyclist saw £1,510 instead of £3,240–£5,750.
- **Self Assessment and side hustle:** no Scottish bands. £55,000 profit in Scotland was understated by £1,650.05.
- **Side hustle:** "You Keep" ignored expenses, overstating the result by the full amount.
- **R&D:** prose said grants are taken off qualifying costs. HMRC: no restriction on subsidised costs under the merged
  scheme or ERIS.
- **Pension sharing:** the slider's 0% end computed 50%, because of `|| 50`.
- **Pension Credit:** one carer amount for a couple where both qualify, no Savings Credit, and no couple age rule.
- **Work from home:** a full office was described as arguably qualifying; gov.uk lists it under "cannot claim".
- **Loft conversion:** hip-to-gable and side dormers shown as needing planning, and a "2.3 m over 50%" headroom rule with no
  UK basis.
- **Underfloor heating:** the widget's running cost was 15 times below the page's own example, and electric always showed
  "Similar cost". A heat pump was said to cost 50-70% less than gas; the page's own rates give 1-26%.
- **Weight loss:** any target was accepted (85 to 30 kg got a plan), and the text described inputs that did not exist,
  endorsed a 2,000 kcal deficit, and listed Ozempic as a weight-loss medicine.
- **Heart rate:** advised an all-out sprint with no heart condition or beta blocker caution.
- **Cooking:** "official UK tablespoon is 17.7 ml"; it is 15 ml.
- **Pond:** stocking sums mixed inches and centimetres, and the liner example was too small for its own pond.
