**Locale:** en-GB · GBP · gov.uk / legislation.gov.uk
**Mode:** sample, with the sample chosen by risk rather than at random. The site had not been revised since the Autumn Budget of 28 October 2026, so the data-currency pass covered every page the Budget touched. The remaining ~70 never-hand-checked pages (building, tools, maths, gardening and similar) were not read in this round.

## Headline

**The site still described the Budget as upcoming three days after it happened, and several figures it presented as settled had been reversed.** 75 pages changed in prose or FAQ and 31 calculator components changed. Nothing here is a formula bug in the current tax year: 2026/27 results are unchanged.

The automated checks could not have found this. All of it was correctly formatted and most of it had been right on 27 October.

## Pre-existing errors found in passing

- **Benefits in kind:** "Section 336 changes this from 2026" was wrong. Mandatory payrolling starts 6 April 2027 for cars, vans, fuel and medical, and 6 April 2028 for most others (OOTLAR 1.24).
- **Tax refund:** said "55p and 25p" for all years, although refunds reach back to the 45p years.
- **State Pension:** displayed "£241.3" instead of £241.30.
- **Business rates:** said the 1p supplement was 2026/27 only (now extended two years).
- **Pay-per-mile:** compared eVED in 2028 with fuel duty at 52.95p, a rate that will have ended; it now uses 60.59p, and VED starts at £210.

## Errors caught in this round's own work

An independent read-only reviewer checked the whole diff against the sources.

- **Pension Tax Allowance:** the working note derived a £93.40 maximum. HM Treasury's explainer says "worth up to £94", £93.28 at exactly £13,036.40. Corrected everywhere, and the State Pension is now the exact £250.70.
- The hub's quick answer implied savings rates were limited to England, Wales and NI; they are UK-wide.
- "No telematics" sat beside the new opt-in eVED Connect.
- The new Updates entry linked a page that does not exist. Found by the browser run.

## Known limits

- **Capital allowances:** the year-by-year table keeps 14% and 40%, because the tool has no date input. A note says so.
- **Business mileage record:** takes trip dates but applies one rate, so it needs per-tax-year logic by April 2027.
- **Landlord, side hustle and similar calculators** stay on 2026/27 with dated notes until the April 2027 rollover.
- **22% charge on cash in stocks and shares ISAs:** the Budget documents do not say what happens to it. The page says unconfirmed.
- **Not verified in this round:** that earnings were the highest triple lock measure. It is not in the primary documents, so the site now states only the 3.9% rise. HVCTS CPI uprating and the 2033 revaluation are older wording that was not re-traced.
- **Other pages:** pattern grep only, so a differently worded stale statement could survive on the 317 calculators not read line by line.
