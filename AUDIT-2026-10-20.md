## Headline

**205 defects on 20/20 pages (11 HIGH, 77 MED, 117 LOW); 11 calculators gave wrong answers for realistic inputs and 9
more described inputs or features they did not have.** The most serious were health and money claims a reader could act on:

- The **alcohol units** page told a 75 kg man the England drink-drive limit was "roughly 3 to 4 units, about 2 pints of
  lager", and in Scotland "a single pint". gov.uk says it is impossible to say how many drinks equal the limit, and the
  figures were wrong anyway: 2 pints of 4% lager is 4.5 units. The tool also never added custom drinks to the total.
- The **BMI** and **ideal weight** tools applied the same bands to everyone. NICE NG246 (January 2025, which replaced NG7)
  sets overweight at 23 and obesity at 27.5 for people of South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African
  or African-Caribbean background. A South Asian adult at BMI 24 was told "Healthy weight".
- **Pension vs ISA** under-credited higher- and additional-rate relief: £250,362 instead of £291,149 at £500 a month for
  20 years at 40%. It also said ISAs do not affect benefits, but ISA savings count as capital for Universal Credit and
  Pension Credit.
- **Agricultural wages:**
  - Scotland's SCQF premium was put into the overtime base: £21.93 an hour, where the Order gives £19.07.
  - Welsh workers got 5.6 weeks' holiday; AWO 2025 Schedule 2 gives 31 days for a five-day week.
  - Hours over 39 in Wales and Scotland were paid at the basic rate.
  - Northern Ireland was missing.
- **Livestock units** used invented coefficients (suckler cow 0.75, ewe 0.08, pony 0.5), although the page cited the RPA
  table (cattle over 2 years 1.0, lowland ewe 0.12, pony 0.8).
- **Student budget** charged rent for 39 weeks of term, so the default "annual surplus" of £1,913 was really about £267.
- **Pregnancy due date** described a cycle-length adjustment and conception-date mode the tool lacked: for LMP 1 Feb 2026
  on a 35-day cycle, the NHS method gives 15 Nov and the tool gave 8 Nov. It also put week 27 in the third trimester.
- **GCSE / Attainment 8** compared scores with the 2025 national average (46.0). DfE published 46.2 for 2026 on
  15 October. The page also quoted "grade 9 at 197 out of 300" for GCSE Maths, which is really AQA's A-level Chemistry
  grade A boundary. The same figure sat on the exam-score page.
- The **profit and loss** FAQ's Corporation Tax figures were wrong: £15,725 / £34,225 where marginal relief gives
  £16,125 / £36,000.

Confirmed current at source (no change needed):
- tax: CGT 18%/24%, AEA £3,000, BADR 18%; CT 19%/25%, 3/200; dividend rates 10.75/35.75/39.35%; Class 4 6%/2%; Maintenance Payments Relief £453;
- pay: NLW £12.71 / £10.85 / £8.00; Welsh AWO grade rates and allowances; SAWB guide rates; SPP/SMP £194.32 and AWE £129;
- students: maintenance loans 2026/27 £9,118 / £10,830 / £14,135;
- exams: UCAS tariff; Attainment 8 method;
- health: NHS 14 units, binge thresholds and 80/50 mg limits; EFSA 2.0/2.5 L; NHS folic acid;
- courts: fees £628 / £321 / £62 (from 13 July 2026).
