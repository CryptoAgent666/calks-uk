## Headline

**146 defects on 20/20 pages (9 HIGH, 62 MED, 75 LOW); 8 calculators gave wrong answers for realistic inputs and 9 more
described inputs, outputs or examples the tool could not produce.** The biggest policy miss: the Congestion Charge page
still said electric cars were exempt, ten months after TfL ended the 100% Cleaner Vehicle Discount (since 2 January
2026 electric cars get 25% off, and only on Auto Pay), and its page title still said £15. Other catches: the MOT
calculator showed the earliest keep-your-date test one day early and broke on month-end dates (gov.uk: a month minus a
day, so 15 May → 16 April, 31 March → 1 March); the season-ticket example applied a 26-30 Railcard discount to an annual
season ticket, which Railcards never give, using London–Brighton fares about a decade old; the investment-return
calculator subtracted inflation month by month and overstated today's-money value by 10% over ten years and 22% over
twenty; the Northern Ireland tuition fee was last year's £4,855 (2026/27: £4,985); the UCAS page gave the Welsh
Baccalaureate certificate only two grades; the double-glazing savings were nearly double the Energy Saving Trust's
current £85 a year for a single-glazed semi; the electricity-cost fridge-freezer preset cost £346 a year; and the EPC
pages presented the 2030 Band C standard for landlords as law, when regulations have not been laid.

Confirmed current at source (no change needed): Ofgem Oct–Dec 2026 cap 26.32p/54.83p and 7.97p/29.68p, £1,723, 0% VAT on
domestic electricity to 31 March 2027 (Jan–Mar 2027 cap due 25 November); maintenance loans 2026/27 (£9,118 / £10,830 /
£14,135, taper and floors reproduce the SLC table to the pound); Master's £13,206 and Doctoral £31,122; MOT maximum fee
£54.85; ULEZ £12.50 and £180 PCN; JISA £9,000; ISA £20,000 with the £12,000 cash limit for under-65s from 6 April 2027
(SI 2026/1018); SFI26 action rates; delinked payments 2026–27 (98% on the first £30,000, 100% above).
