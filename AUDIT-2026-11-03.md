## Headline

**126 defects on 20/20 pages (12 HIGH, 60 MED, 54 LOW). 17 calculators gave wrong results for some inputs,** plus Annual Tax Summary outside the sample. All were worked on. The most serious:

- **Annuity:** rates were 17% to 32% below current best-buy rates (£12,400 where Hargreaves Lansdown's table gives £16,422 on £200,000 at 65).
- **Inheritance Tax on pensions:** a pension left to a spouse reduced the non-pension estate under today's rules too. £800,000 to children and a £500,000 pension to a spouse showed £0 now and +£120,000 from 2027; both are £120,000.
- **Side hustle:** Class 4 NI ignored the annual maximum in regulation 100. Salary £60,000 and profit £30,000 showed £1,045.80, where the maximum gives £348.60. Annual Tax Summary had the same fault.
- **Housing Benefit:** offered awards to people who cannot make a new claim, including mixed-age couples, and used the post-2021 pensioner rate for everyone. Savings, children and non-dependants had no inputs.
- **Working hours:** couples were tested against the wrong thresholds in both directions.
- **SIPP:** no warning above £60,000 or the earnings limit (£6,000 a month net showed full relief on £90,000), and higher-rate relief applied to the whole contribution.
- **Zero hours:** holiday pay sat outside gross, tax and NI, and NI ran on annual pay, so uneven hours paid nothing (40 hours for 24 weeks: £511.49 due, £0 shown).
- **Plan 4:** years and write-off ignored interest (£40,000 salary and £15,000 balance: about £18,330 written off at year 30, shown as repaid in about 27 years).
- **Home buying:** England and NI SDLT applied silently to Scotland and Wales, with no additional-property option (second home at £300,000: £20,000 tax, £5,000 shown).

## Known limits

- **Housing Benefit** leaves out premiums and earnings disregards, and the widget says so. Spare-room treatment for supported housing is left for the council to confirm. A mixed-age couple where neither reached SPA before April 2021 uses £363.25, which Schedule 3 does not literally cover.
- **Working hours:** the "Light touch" label was not seen at a source. A couple claiming as a single person (reg 90(3)(b)) is not modelled.
- **Annuity** rates are best of market on one date, not typical quotes, and don't extend past the age-75 row.
- **Solicitor:** searches (£300), bank transfer (£40) and the £2,500 probate fee are labelled rough estimates. Buying and selling share one fee scale.
- **Home buying:** non-tax costs in Scotland and NI use England and Wales assumptions, and the page says so. The LTT £40,000 floor comes from the site's LTT component, not gov.wales.
- **Plan 4** projects a full 30 years from today with thresholds frozen, and says so.
- **Winter Fuel:** the statutory income definition is not stated, only gov.uk's wording.
- **Freelance and side hustle:** "£200 to £500" incorporation and "UTR 2-4 weeks" stay unverified. gov.uk's payment on account test is "less than £1,000", while the site says "over £1,000", which differs only at exactly £1,000.
- **Rollover without a guard:** the EV December 2026 start date and its National Living Wage constant.
- **Fuel prices** stay at the DESNZ week of 12 October, labelled as such.
- **A reviewer sent one POST to HMRC's income checker** (tax-year choice only, no personal data), which returned a validation error, and was told not to submit forms.
